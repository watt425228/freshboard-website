Getting started

What is Freshboard?
Freshboard is digital menu board software for restaurants. You keep your menu in one place, and when you press Publish, the change appears on the TV menu boards in your restaurant and on your website menu at the same time. Google Business Profile menu updates are coming soon.
Who is Freshboard for?
Freshboard is for restaurants, cafés, bars, bakeries, food halls and quick-service counters that show their menu on TVs, from a single location to many.
How do I get started with Freshboard?
Create an account at app.freshboard.org, type in your menu, choose a look for each screen, and press Publish. Your 14-day free trial starts straight away, and you don't need a card.
How long does it take to set up?
Most restaurants can type in their menu and set up their screens in an afternoon. After that, changing a price takes seconds.
Do I need technical skills to use Freshboard?
No. If you can edit a document, you can use Freshboard. You type your dishes and prices, pick a design, and press Publish.
Can I try Freshboard before I pay?
Yes. Every account is free for 14 days with everything included, and you don't need a card to start. Start your free trial.

Menu boards

How do my TV menu boards get updated?
When you press Publish, every screen showing that menu updates within seconds. Nobody has to touch the TVs.
How many screens can I have?
As many as you like. Every screen at a location is included in the $39.99 monthly price for that location.
Can each screen show a different menu?
Yes. Show food on one TV and drinks on the next, or pick the exact sections each screen shows.
What do the menu boards look like?
Choose from 110 designs and 50 fonts, and add your logo. Each board sizes itself to fill the screen, works on TVs hung horizontally or vertically, and can be a daily specials board with a photo of each dish. See all features.
Can I keep the menu design I already have?
Yes. Upload your finished menu as a PNG, JPG or WebP image. Freshboard reads its dishes, prices and descriptions into your menu, and when you change a name, price or description, the new words appear where the old ones were printed, in the same size and colour. You can also use your image as a background and have your menu set over it.
What if the reader gets a word wrong on my uploaded menu?
Correct it in the app and choose “The print already says this”. The item is fixed everywhere else, and your menu board is left exactly as it was printed.
Can you design my menu board for me?
Yes. Choose “Design it for me” on any screen's Design page. For $149, paid once, tell us the look you want and add any pictures to work from, and our designers make a finished menu for that screen, usually within 5 business days. Its names, prices and descriptions stay editable from the app.
Can I show daily specials with photos?
Yes. Upload a photo of each dish and turn any screen into a specials board that shows them as big picture cards.
Can I see a menu board before it goes live?
Yes. Preview any menu on a TV-shaped screen before you publish, so you see exactly what customers will see.
What happens to my menu boards if the internet goes down?
Your screens keep showing the menu they have, and catch up as soon as they're back online.

Updating your menu

How do I change a price on my menu?
Change the price in Freshboard and press Publish. Every menu board that shows that dish updates within seconds, along with your menu page and your website menu.
What happens when an item sells out?
Mark it sold out and it's crossed off on your screens and your website until you switch it back. It takes one tap.
Can I make changes without them going live straight away?
Yes. Edits wait until you press Publish, so you can make several changes, check them, and send them out together. A half-typed price never reaches a customer.
Can different locations have different prices?
Yes. Share one menu across every location, and set a different price, or mark a dish sold out, at any one of them.
Can my menu change for breakfast, lunch and happy hour?
Yes. Set your dayparts once, each with its own menu, days and times, and your screens switch on their own. They can also show countdowns such as “Happy hour ends in 20 min” with a promo line, and your website menu puts what's being served now first.
Can I see what was changed and when?
Yes. Freshboard keeps a record of every publish: what changed, when, and whether each screen and your website took it.

Your website and Google

How do I put my menu on my website?
Two ways. Link to the menu page Freshboard makes for each location, or paste two lines of code into your website and your menu appears there, in your site's own fonts and colours. Both update every time you publish.
Will my website menu match my menu boards?
Yes. Your screens and your website menu come from the same menu and update with the same Publish, so they always show the same prices.
Can search engines read my Freshboard menu page?
Yes. Your menu page includes structured data that tells search engines which lines are dishes and prices, so they can show it as a menu.
Does Freshboard update my Google Business Profile menu?
Google Business Profile menu updates are coming soon. Once they're available, your Google menu will be replaced every time you publish, so Search and Maps show today's prices.
Does Freshboard update delivery apps like DoorDash or Uber Eats?
Not yet. Today Freshboard updates your TV menu boards and your website menu, and Google Business Profile updates are coming soon.

Add-ons

What add-ons does Freshboard have?
Three optional add-ons: allergen and dietary lookup, a menu A/B tester, and social posts. Turn any of them on from the Billing page in your account.
How does the allergen and dietary lookup work?
Customers scan a QR code at your counter and filter your menu by gluten-free, nut-free, vegan and more. Every answer comes from one ingredient list you keep, so dishes made with the same sauce always agree. A dish without ingredients listed is shown as unknown, and customers are told to ask your staff.
How does the menu A/B tester work?
Try a new name, a different photo or a better spot for a dish. Your boards show each version on alternating days, you add the day's sales, and Freshboard tells you which version sells more and whether the difference is likely to be real.
Can Freshboard post to social media for me?
Freshboard writes the post for you. Link your Instagram, Facebook, X and TikTok, choose a new dish or a promotion, and it drafts a caption for each platform. You edit it and share it: X opens with the post written, and for the others the caption is copied and the picture is ready.

Pricing and billing

How much does Freshboard cost?
$39.99 a month for each location, with unlimited screens, edits and publishes. See pricing.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every account is free for 14 days with everything included, and you don't need a card to start. If you subscribe early, you keep the rest of your free days.
Are there setup fees or contracts?
No. You pay $39.99 a month for each location, and nothing else unless you order a custom menu design or turn on an add-on. There's no contract.
How do I pay for Freshboard?
By card, through Stripe. Card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored by Freshboard.
What happens to my bill when I add or close a location?
Your bill follows, prorated to the day. Add or close a location any time.
How do I cancel Freshboard?
Cancel any time from the Billing page. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and there are no cancellation fees.
What happens if a payment fails?
Stripe tries again over the following days, and you can keep publishing meanwhile. If it still can't be collected, publishing pauses until billing is sorted out, and your screens keep showing the last menu you published.

Your account and data

Is my information secure with Freshboard?
Yes. Freshboard is only reachable over encrypted connections, passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and card details are handled by Stripe. Read the privacy policy.
I forgot my password. What do I do?
Choose “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page and enter your email. You'll get a link to choose a new one, which works once, for an hour.
Who owns my menu content?
You do. Your menus, prices, photos and logos stay yours; Freshboard only uses them to run the service for you. Read the terms.
How do I delete my Freshboard account?
Email info@freshboard.org and we'll delete your account and your content within 30 days, apart from anything we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.
