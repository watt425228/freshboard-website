Everything your menu needs, from one screen

You keep one menu. Freshboard puts it on your TVs, your website and your Google listing, and keeps all three right as prices, dishes and the time of day change.

Menu screens

Any TV with a web browser works: a smart TV, a streaming stick or a small computer.

Nothing to install
Open the screen's link on the TV once. It updates itself from then on, and picks up where it left off if the internet drops.
50 designs and 50 fonts
Add your logo and choose a look. The menu sizes itself to fill the screen, however many dishes you have.
A menu for each screen
Food on one TV, drinks on the next, or pick the exact sections each screen shows.
Hung the usual way or on its side
Choose horizontal or vertical for each TV, and turn it the other way if it's mounted upside down.
Daily specials boards
Upload a photo of each dish and show your specials as big picture cards.
See it before it's live
Preview any menu on a TV-shaped screen before you publish.

Keep the menu you already have

Works with PNG, JPG and WebP images of your finished menu.

Your prices load in by themselves
Upload your finished menu. Freshboard reads its dishes, prices and descriptions so you can edit them like any other menu.
Changes printed in place
Change a price and the new one appears where the old one was printed, in the same lettering and colour. The rest of your design stays exactly as it was.
Or use it as a backdrop
Keep your background artwork and let Freshboard set the menu on top of it.
Or let us design it
Order a custom menu design for any screen for a one-time fee. It arrives ready to use, with its prices linked to your menu.

Breakfast to late night

Happy hour ends in 20 min $5 drafts and half-price wings

Menus that change with the clock
Set breakfast, lunch, happy hour and late night once. Your screens switch at the right minute, every day, with nobody touching them.
Countdowns that sell
Screens can announce what's coming ("Happy hour starts in 15 min") and count down what's ending, with a promo line of your own.
Your website keeps time too
Your menu page puts what's being served now at the top, or shows only that, with a link to the full menu.
Weekdays, weekends, past midnight
Each daypart has its own days and hours, and late night can run past 2am.

Your website and Google

A menu page that's always current
Every location gets its own menu page to link from anywhere: your site, your social profiles, a QR code on the table.
Or on your own site
Paste two lines into the website you already have and your menu appears there, in your site's fonts and colours.
Read correctly by search engines
Your menu page tells search engines which lines are dishes and prices, so they can show it as a menu.
Google Business Profile
Connect your listing once and its menu is replaced every time you publish, so Search and Maps show today's prices.

One menu, many locations

Prices that differ by location
Share one menu everywhere and let the airport location charge a dollar more for the same beer.
Sold out in one place
Run out of something downtown and it's crossed off there, while the other locations keep selling it.
Nothing changes until you publish
Edits wait until you press Publish, so a half-typed price never reaches a customer.
A record of every publish
See what changed, when, and whether each screen, your site and Google took it.

Add-ons, when you want them

Turn any of these on from your account. They stay out of your way until you do.

Allergen and dietary lookup

Customers scan a QR code at the counter and filter your menu by gluten-free, nut-free, vegan and more.

Every answer comes from one ingredient list you keep, so two dishes made with the same sauce always agree.

Menu A/B tester

Try a new name, a different photo or a better spot for a dish. Your boards show each version on alternating days.

Add the day's sales and Freshboard tells you which version sells more, and whether the difference is real.

Social posts

Link your Instagram, Facebook, X and TikTok, then post about a new dish or a promotion.

Freshboard drafts the caption for each one in the way that platform reads best, ready for you to edit and share.
