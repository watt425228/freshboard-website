Set up takes an afternoon. After that, a price change takes seconds.

  2. Choose where it showsPick a design for each TV and decide which menu it shows: food on one screen, drinks on the next. Add your menu to your website with two lines of code, or link to your Freshboard menu page.
  3. PublishChange a price, mark a dish sold out or add a special, then press Publish. Every screen and your website update together.

Questions restaurants ask

Ask us something else

What is Freshboard?
Freshboard is digital menu board software for restaurants. You keep your menu in one place, and when you publish a change it appears on the TV menu boards in your restaurant and on your website menu at the same time. Google Business Profile menu updates are coming soon.
Who is Freshboard for?
Restaurants, cafés, bars, bakeries, food halls and quick-service counters that show their menu on TVs, from a single location to many.
How do I change the prices on my menu boards?
Change the price in Freshboard and press Publish. Every menu board that shows that dish updates within seconds, along with your menu page and your website menu. Nothing changes until you publish, so a half-typed price never reaches a customer.
What do the menu boards look like?
Choose from 110 designs and 50 fonts and add your logo. Each board sizes itself to fill the screen, works on TVs hung horizontally or vertically, and can show a menu of its own: food on one TV, drinks on the next.
Can my menu boards change for breakfast, lunch and happy hour?
Yes. Set your dayparts once, each with its own menu, days and times, and your screens switch on their own. They can also show countdowns such as “Happy hour ends in 20 min” with a promo line.
How much does Freshboard cost?
$39.99 a month for each location, with unlimited screens, edits and publishes. It's free for 14 days, you don't need a card to start, and you can cancel any time.
Does Freshboard work for more than one location?
Yes. Share one menu across every location, and set a different price or mark a dish sold out at any one of them.
Can customers check allergens and dietary needs?
With the optional allergen and dietary lookup, customers scan a QR code at the counter and filter your menu by gluten-free, nut-free, vegan and more. Every answer comes from one ingredient list you keep, so dishes made with the same sauce always agree.
Does Freshboard update my Google Business Profile menu?
Google Business Profile menu updates are coming soon. Once they're available, your Google menu will be replaced every time you publish, so Search and Maps show today's prices.
