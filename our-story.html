Our story

Why we built Freshboard, and why one menu change shouldn't turn into ten separate jobs.

Menus never sit still

Restaurant menus never sit still. Prices move with food costs, a special sells out, a seasonal dish comes back, a supplier changes, and suddenly the menu you published last month is wrong.

That isn't a sign that anything is broken. It's what running a restaurant looks like. The price of eggs jumps and breakfast follows it. The fish that came in this morning isn't the fish on the menu. A dish that was meant to run for a week turns into a regular. Good restaurants change their menus all the time, because their kitchens, their suppliers and their customers keep changing too.

The hard part is making it everywhere

The hard part isn't making the change. It's making it everywhere. Your website, your Google Business Profile, your delivery apps, your social pages, the menu board over the counter: each one has its own login, its own format, and its own way of falling out of date.

So a price change that takes five seconds to decide takes an afternoon to finish. Log in here, find the right page there, retype the same numbers, export another PDF, and hope you didn't miss one. Owners end up spending hours chasing down every copy, and customers still find an old price or a dish you stopped serving months ago. They can't tell which copy is out of date. They only know the menu said one thing and the counter said another.

Why does one menu change turn into ten separate jobs?

We saw this up close working with restaurants, and we kept asking that same question. Every owner had a system of their own: a list of places to update taped by the register, the one person on staff who knew the website password, a menu board that only got fixed when a customer pointed at it. None of it was the owner's fault. The tools were simply never built to work together.

So we built a better way

Update your menu once, click one button, and it changes everywhere Freshboard is connected. No more hunting through logins, no more mismatched prices, no more “that's not what it said online.”

Here is what that means today:

  • Your menu boards. The TVs over your counter update within seconds of pressing Publish, in any of 110 designs.
  • Your website. A menu page for each location that's always current, or your menu placed inside the website you already have.
  • Every location. One shared menu, with a different price or a sold-out dish wherever you need one.
  • Every part of the day. Breakfast, lunch, happy hour and late-night menus that switch on their own, with countdowns that tell customers what's ending soon.
  • Your social pages. With the Social add-on, Freshboard writes the post about your new dish for Instagram, Facebook, X and TikTok, ready for you to share.
  • Your Google listing, coming soon. Search and Maps will show the same prices as your counter, every time you publish.

Nothing changes until you press Publish, so a half-typed price never reaches a customer, and every change you make is recorded, so you can always see what changed and when.
