Questions

Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every account is free for 14 days, with everything included, and you don't need a card to start. If you subscribe early, you keep the rest of your free days.
Are there setup fees or contracts?
No. You pay $39.99 a month for each location, and nothing else unless you turn on an add-on.
What happens if the internet goes down?
Your screens keep showing the menu they have, and catch up as soon as they're back online.
Does it work with my website?
Yes. Link to your Freshboard menu page, or paste two lines into any website that lets you add code.
Can I cancel?
Any time, from your account. There's no contract.
Can each location have its own prices?
Yes. Share one menu and set a different price, or mark something sold out, at any location.
