Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

Freshboard is run by Watt Consulting, based in [STATE] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we keep when restaurants use Freshboard, and what happens when their customers look at a Freshboard menu. We don't sell personal information and we don't show ads.

What we keep about restaurants

  • Your account: your restaurant's name, your email address, your password (stored only as a secure one-way hash, never as the password itself), and the names, addresses and phone numbers of your locations if you add them.
  • Your menus and what you upload: dishes, prices, descriptions, photos, logos, menu designs, ingredient lists, daypart schedules, the sales figures you enter for menu tests, social profile names and post drafts.
  • Your screens: the settings for each screen and when it last connected.
  • Billing: your subscription status and the reference Stripe gives us. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Google: if you connect your Google Business Profile, the Google account email and a sign-in credential, stored encrypted, used only to update your listing's menu.
  • Sign-in safety: your IP address is used briefly to limit repeated sign-in and sign-up attempts. It's held in memory, not saved. Freshboard doesn't keep a log of the pages you visit.

Cookies

Freshboard uses one cookie, to keep you signed in. It isn't used for tracking. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

Your customers

People who look at your menu screens, menu pages, website menu or allergen page aren't asked for any personal information, and we don't track them. Like any website, their device connects to our servers to load the menu, and to Google Fonts to load the lettering, which means Google sees their IP address.

How we use information

  • To run Freshboard: store your menus and publish them where you ask.
  • To bill you, and to send emails you need, such as password reset links and important notices about your account.
  • To keep Freshboard secure and to help when you contact us.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run Freshboard, and only what each needs:

  • Hostinger, which hosts our servers and delivers our email.
  • Stripe, which handles payments.
  • Google, if you connect your Business Profile, receives your menu. Google Fonts supplies the lettering on menu pages.
  • jsDelivr supplies the menu-reading software your browser uses when you upload a finished menu. The reading happens on your own computer.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people or Freshboard from harm. If Freshboard is ever sold or merged, your information would move with it under this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your content within 30 days. Backups are kept for up to 14 days, so a deleted item can remain in a backup for that long. We keep billing records as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Security

Freshboard is only reachable over encrypted connections. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and Google credentials are encrypted. Access to our servers is limited to the people who run Freshboard. No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach affects your information, we'll tell you.

Your choices

You can see and change most of your information in the app. Email us to get a copy of your information, to correct it, or to have your account deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR; we'll honour them when they apply.

Children

Freshboard is a service for businesses and isn't meant for children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell restaurants by email or in the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your information: ryan@wattconsulting.net.
